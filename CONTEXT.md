# RngHelpdesk

Tracks the members of a RuneScape clan's Discord community: who they are on Discord, which RSNs they play under, and the clan points that decide their rank.

## Language

**Member**:
A person in the clan, identified by their Discord account, who can have RSNs linked and earn clan points.
_Avoid_: User, player

**Deactivated member**:
A member no longer in the clan, whether they left or were removed, whose clan points, RSNs and history are kept; reactivating them restores all of it.
_Avoid_: Deleted, banned, removed member

### RSNs

**RSN**:
A RuneScape account a member plays under, known by its in-game name; a member can have several.
_Avoid_: RuneScape account, username, character, alt

**Link**:
To record that an RSN belongs to a member; the reverse is **Unlink**.
_Avoid_: Add, remove, delink

**Rename**:
To record that a linked RSN changed its in-game name, keeping it the same RSN rather than unlinking one and linking another.

### Clan points

**Clan points**:
The running total a member earns for clan activity, adjusted up or down by staff with a stated reason; "points" is fine as shorthand.
_Avoid_: Score, XP, CP

### Ranks

**Rank**:
The standing a member holds in the clan — either a point rank or a staff rank.
_Avoid_: Role, app role, tier

**Point rank**:
A rank earned by reaching its rank threshold in clan points, from Bronze up to Zenyte.

**Staff rank**:
A rank assigned to run the clan — Administrator, Deputy Owner or Owner — that overrides the member's point rank regardless of their clan points.
_Avoid_: SuperAdministrator, admin role

**Rank threshold**:
The number of clan points a member needs to hold a given point rank.
